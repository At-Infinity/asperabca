About the Fest

Welcome to ASPERA IT Fest 2026

ASPERA IT Fest is the premier technological extravaganza of the Department of Computer Applications, Sree KeralaVarma College, Thrissur. This annual festival brings together the most brilliant minds for a celebration of innovation, technical prowess, and creative expression.

This year's edition features an exciting blend of technical challenges, creative competitions, and thrilling tournaments. From keyboard assembling to traditional ramp walks, we have something for every tech enthusiast and creative mind.

Our mission is to provide a dynamic platform for students to showcase their diverse talents, learn through competition, and network with peers who share their passion for technology and innovation.
