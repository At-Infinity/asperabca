The Legacy of ASPERA

Where Tech Meets Talent

ASPERA is the premier technological extravaganza hosted by the Department of Computer Applications. We bring together the brightest minds across Kerala for a high-octane celebration of code, creativity, and competition.

From blistering keyboard assembly races to solving complex digital crimes, ASPERA '26 offers a battleground for every tech enthusiast to prove their mettle and network with fellow innovators.
